03

How I work

Working across strategy, design, and engineering to deliver the most value to users.

I care as much about why we're building, and whether we can build it, as I do about the craft of the design itself.

01

Strategy first

Start from product strategy and the real user problem — not a feature request handed down.

02

Engineering reality

Partner closely with engineering to understand what's possible, and what it costs to build.

03

Ship, then iterate

Work doesn't stop at the first build — I go back to users, learn what's working (and what's not), and keep iterating.

About

I'm Annie — a staff product designer at Moderne, working at the frontier of agent experience, developer platforms, and design systems.

I pair user research with interaction design and design systems, and partner closely with engineering to bring the work to life. I care about the thinking behind the screens — the real problem, the system, and the decisions that get a team to a good answer together — and I bring a curious, slightly playful approach to serious software.
