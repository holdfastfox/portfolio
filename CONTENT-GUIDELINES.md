# Content Guidelines

This repository exists to document my professional accomplishments, design thinking, and career growth. Its purpose is reflection, storytelling, and portfolio development — **not** replication of proprietary company assets.

These guidelines apply to every file in this repo, and to any AI assistant working in it. When in doubt, follow the [Public Test](#the-public-test) and the [Default Behavior](#default-behavior).

---

## Non-negotiable rules

**Never include:**

- Source code owned by an employer or client
- Internal documentation
- Roadmaps
- Strategy documents
- Product requirements documents
- Customer data
- Customer names
- Employee names
- Slack conversations
- Meeting transcripts
- Internal emails
- Financial information
- Revenue information
- Non-public metrics
- Internal screenshots
- Proprietary diagrams
- Exported Figma files
- Internal GitHub issues
- Internal tickets
- Internal AI conversations that contain confidential information

---

## Portfolio transformation rule

When describing work, transform specific information into generalized information.

| Specific (don't use) | Generalized (use) |
|---|---|
| "Moderne Recipe Catalog" | "Large-scale developer documentation platform" |
| "7,500 recipe pages" | "Thousands of technical content pages" |
| "Customer X" | "Enterprise customer" |
| Specific metrics | Relative outcomes |
| Employee names | Roles only |
| Internal screenshots | Recreated conceptual examples |
| Real UI | Portfolio-safe illustrative UI |

---

## Story over artifact

**Capture:** problems, constraints, decisions, tradeoffs, leadership, collaboration, design rationale, technical-feasibility considerations, business-strategy considerations, outcomes, and lessons learned.

**Do not capture:** proprietary implementation details, confidential business information, or intellectual property.

---

## AI usage rules

**AI may help:** summarize work, organize information, draft case studies, create placeholder examples, generate illustrative visuals, reconstruct timelines, and identify patterns.

**AI must never:** recreate confidential company assets, infer missing confidential information, generate fake company metrics, generate realistic reproductions of proprietary systems, or reveal internal processes that are not publicly known.

---

## The Public Test

Before saving or publishing anything, ask:

> "If my manager, legal counsel, a future employer, and the original company all reviewed this content, would they agree that it communicates my contribution without exposing company intellectual property?"

If the answer is uncertain, remove or generalize the content.

---

## Default behavior

When in doubt:

- Generalize
- Abstract
- Anonymize
- Simplify

Prefer protecting confidentiality over preserving specificity.

The goal is to document **how I think and what I contributed** — not to reproduce what the company owns.
