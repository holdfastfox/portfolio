About

I'm Annie, a staff product designer working on agent experience, developer platforms, and design systems.

I started as a coder and tinkerer. I asked my parents for Paint Shop Pro 7 as a birthday present and hosted my friends' blogs on a domain built with iframes and retro HTML. I thought I was the coolest. A career as a classical musician came first. Joining Pivotal reignited my love of web design. It lets me empathize with users, work with pixels, and sit next to engineers all day. Eight-plus years in, I still work that way: research, interaction design, systems, in partnership with engineering. On the side: logo systems, e-commerce UX, small-business rebrands. Old habits.
