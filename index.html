How I work

Start from the real problem. Design the whole system. Ship with curiosity intact.

A process that holds up at enterprise scale, and stays human anyway.

01

Understand

I pair user research with the real request and make the complexity legible before designing anything.

02

Design the system

Interaction models and design systems, not isolated screens. I show my work as I go, mess and all.

03

Ship & learn

I partner with engineering to prototype, test, and ship — and curiosity doesn't clock out at launch.

About

I'm Annie — a staff product designer at Moderne, working at the frontier of agent experience, developer platforms, and design systems.

I pair user research with interaction design and design systems, and partner closely with engineering — design that doesn't ship isn't really design. I care about the thinking behind the screens: the real problem, the decisions, what it takes to get a team to a good answer. I bring a curious, slightly playful approach to software that's pretty serious about itself. On the side: logo systems, e-commerce UX, small-business rebrands. Old habits.
